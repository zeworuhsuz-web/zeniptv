# Privacy Policy for ZenIPTV Player

**Last Updated:** November 8, 2026

ZenIPTV ("we", "our", or "the application") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the ZenIPTV Player application on Windows.

### 1. Information Collection and Storage
ZenIPTV is a client-side media player. 
- **No Personal Data Collected:** We do not collect, transmit, track, or store any personal information, browsing history, or identity data.
- **Local Storage Only:** Any playlist URLs (M3U), Xtream Codes credentials (server URL, username, password), favorite channels, and playback settings entered by the user are stored strictly locally on the user's personal device using secure local storage / IndexedDB.
- **No Remote Telemetry or Tracking:** The application does not contain third-party trackers, telemetry analytics, or advertising SDKs.

### 2. Network Communication
- All media streaming requests (HLS, MP4, TS) are initiated directly from the user's device to the user's specified stream server or IPTV provider.
- ZenIPTV does not route or intercept your video traffic through any remote third-party servers.

### 3. Third-Party Services
ZenIPTV does not share, sell, or disclose any user data to third parties.

### 4. Children's Privacy
ZenIPTV does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13.
